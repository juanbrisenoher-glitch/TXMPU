Road Map

Study Plan

Week 1
Giveback Mastery
Memorize all 7 giveback plans ranked by amount. Know MOOP, specialist, OTC. Drill till instant recall. Focus on Devoted's $184.70 and HS Full at $185.
Week 2
D-SNP Deep Dive
OTC, dental, hearing, transport per carrier. Know QMB/FBDE/SLMB/QI eligibility. Wellcare plan differences. Remember: Devoted Dual has NO transport.
Week 3
C-SNP + Exceptions
C-SNP costs, hospital per-day copays, ER/ambulance. Note: Alignment SmartSavings hearing = exam only, no aids. HS Full Savings hearing = $399–$1,800/aid copay.
Week 4
Full Speed Drills
Speed rounds, full quizzes, match games daily. Use the Compare tool for client scenarios. Target 90%+ accuracy before AEP.
Daily Routine
✅ 10 flashcards every morning
📝 One full quiz per day (filter to your weak carrier)
⚡ 3 speed rounds on weak areas
🧠 Review Memory Aids before bed
🎯 One match game daily for number recall
⚖️ One comparison session for client scenario prep
